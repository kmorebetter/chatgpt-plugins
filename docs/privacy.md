# Privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026. Publisher: Better Story, operated by Kerry Morrison.

This policy covers The Compiler, GTM Engine, and Buyer / Target Book skills-only plugins distributed from this repository.

## Information used

The workflows use the task description, business context, source documents, results, and progress information you choose to provide to ChatGPT or Codex. If you authorize research through an available tool or connected service, that service may process the search terms or documents needed for your task. Do not provide passwords, API keys, payment-card information, government identifiers, protected health information, or other restricted data.

## Purpose and recipients

The information is used to clarify your task, create plans or research briefs, and assess results. These plugins contain instructions and packaged references only. They have no publisher-operated server, telemetry, external upload endpoint, or automatic reporting to Kerry Morrison. The publisher does not receive your conversations through these plugins. OpenAI and any tools you choose to use process information under their own policies and account settings.

## Retention and controls

The publisher retains no plugin conversation data because none is received. Conversation and connected-service retention is governed by those providers. You can choose what material to provide, decline a connection, remove the plugin, and manage or delete conversation records through your OpenAI account. Generated files or progress records saved in your workspace remain there until you remove them. The workflows do not silently save information to another service.

## Support

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you include. Send redacted examples, not confidential records. Support messages are used to respond and fix reported problems, are not sold, and are retained for up to 90 days after resolution unless legal obligations require longer retention. You can request deletion by emailing kmo@betterstory.co. Email providers process these messages to deliver the support service.

## Changes

Material changes will be dated on this page and reflected in a package update. Questions or privacy requests: kmo@betterstory.co.

# Buyer / Target Book

Prepare a company brief before a sales conversation or a bounded shortlist of organizations for a defined offer. Checks identity, cites deciding evidence, explains possible fit, and separates facts, inferences, hypotheses, and unknowns. Public signals do not establish purchase intent. Works with available authorized research tools or supplied documents. Does not collect private contact data, send outreach, or update a CRM.

## Try it

- Prepare a company brief from these sources before my sales meeting.
- Screen these companies against my offer and explain which need more verification.
- Build a five-account shortlist with evidence, exclusions, and discovery questions.

No separate publisher account is required. Use the plugin in a supported ChatGPT or Codex session. For a new session, provide any prior working record or evidence you want to continue from.

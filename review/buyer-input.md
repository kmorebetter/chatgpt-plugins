# Fictional buyer research fixture

All organizations below are invented for testing. No website was fetched. These are supplied source excerpts, not verified live business facts. Publication and retrieval dates are unknown unless explicitly stated.

Offer: a missing-document checklist for independent Canadian bookkeeping firms with 2–10 staff. Required fit: Canada, bookkeeping services, independent business, 2–10 staff. Exclude US-only businesses, software vendors, and national accounting chains. Unknown staff size requires verification. Do not infer budget, buying authority, technology stack, consent to contact, or purchase timing.

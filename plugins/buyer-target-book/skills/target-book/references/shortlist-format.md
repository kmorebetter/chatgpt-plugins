# Target book format

Lead with the recommended first account to research and why, including its most important unresolved question.

State scope: offer, geography, requested count, evidence window, source availability, must-haves and exclusions.

| Organization | Identity | Core fit evidence | Optional signal | Status | Why this position | Main unknown | Next verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Statuses: research priority, needs verification, exclude. These are research states, not purchase-intent labels.

For each research priority, include: what the business does, the relevant evidence, why the offer might matter, the assumption to test, the professional function to involve, and one discovery question. Do not pad with generic company summaries.

Report counts of distinct candidates reviewed and each status, with deduplication notes. Source ledger columns: ID, publisher/title, link or supplied file and passage, stated publication date or unknown, actual retrieval date when fetched, supported claim. Label supplied facts as supplied. End with scope limits and a next step. All output remains a draft for the user unless they separately authorize publication.

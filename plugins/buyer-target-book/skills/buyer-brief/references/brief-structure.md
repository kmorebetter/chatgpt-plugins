# Company brief structure

1. **Why this conversation may be useful.** A clear recommendation tied to the user's offer, with the main caveat.
2. **Identity.** Organization, official site or supplied identifier, location if relevant, confidence and any unresolved matching issues.
3. **What we know.** A concise table of relevant claim, evidence/source, date, and evidence status.
4. **Possible fit.** Documented situation, proposed value, unknown assumption, and question that would test it.
5. **Who to involve.** Relevant functions or verified professional roles. Buying authority unknown unless explicitly established.
6. **Questions and opening.** Three to five practical discovery questions; an optional short opening grounded in facts.
7. **Limits and next step.** Missing sources, stale or contradictory evidence, and one bounded verification action.
8. **Sources.** Ledger with IDs, links or file references, publisher, stated publication date, actual retrieval date where applicable, and supported claims.

For a quick meeting brief, compress the structure. Do not fill empty sections with invented facts. Use the user's requested format and language when specified.

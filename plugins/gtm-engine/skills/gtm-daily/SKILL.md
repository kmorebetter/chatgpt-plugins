# Daily check-in

Read [working method](references/working-method.md). Recover the plan and latest log. If the plan is missing, ask for it or offer to create it. Do not infer an installed reminder from a saved plan.

Ask for the day being reported, actual counts, and one blocker, one useful question at a time. Skip known answers. Track first contacts, follow-ups, substantive conversations, held calls, started pilots, and payments per known channel. A booking is not a held call. If only combined totals are supplied, record a Combined row; never split evenly or attribute to the busiest channel. Distinguish real zero from not reported.

Append a dated entry without replacing earlier entries. Corrections are appended with a pointer to the corrected entry. Avoid duplicate day/channel counts. For catch-up, record unknown days as not reported if the user cannot remember; do not force a long interview. Use the actual recording date and separately identify the activity date. In unattended mode, do not invent activity or ask unanswerable questions.

Propose at most three manageable actions from the existing plan and any explicitly due follow-up. Log structural concerns for review rather than silently changing the audience or offer. Present the updated log section and continuation record. State saved versus shown accurately.

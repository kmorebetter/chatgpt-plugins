# Start or resume

Read [working method](references/working-method.md).

Start from the user's product description and authorized product material. Briefly restate what the product does and the problem it solves. If a record exists, show the last confirmed decision and next stage, then resume the requested part instead of repeating intake.

Recover: the product in one sentence; who uses it today and how often; what changes in their work; what they tried before; actual payment or usage evidence; the user's reachable network; time and budget ceiling; boundaries they do not want to cross; their own words and optional sign-off for outreach. Ask at most nine relevant questions, one at a time, skipping known answers. If nobody uses it yet, record that honestly and make the first task validation, not assumed demand.

Output the context section and the next missing stage in the GTM record. Preserve distinctions between observed use and hypothetical customers. Do not start research or create an outreach plan before the audience and capacity are sufficiently clear.
